# Changelog

All notable changes to CrystOD, newest first.

## v0.4.6

Highlights (details in the entries below):

- `crystod-phonon --unfold`: the phonons of a distorted phase unfolded onto the Brillouin zone of its high-symmetry parent, with the transformation and the origin shift that `crystod-group --supergroup-cif` finds for the two structures, the parent irrep of every unfolded level, every arm of the stars of the path vertices, the average over the orientation domains, and the parent k and irreps the child Γ modes come from; `crystod.phonon.unfold` and its companions in Python. Test sections run 1-53 (51: `--unfold`, 52: `--visualize`, 53: `crystod-slab`).
- `crystod-phonon --visualize`: an interactive 3D viewer of the phonon modes at the special points of the k path, grouped by irrep: one standalone HTML page with the dispersion, the mode table of every point with the ISO-IR labels (Mulliken symbol and IR/Raman activity at Γ, imaginary levels in red) and the displacement pattern of every mode as arrows and as an animation, next to `band_vector.yaml`, phonopy's `band.yaml` with eigenvectors; `crystod.phonon.phonon_viewer_data` in Python. With `--all-irreps`, the pair of `--irreps --all-irreps`, the page (`phonon_visualize_all.html`) also shows the midpoint of every segment of the path with its line irreps.
- `crystod-phonon --irreps` now also draws the phonon dispersion with the ISO-IR label of every level at the special points of the path (`phonon_irreps.pdf`, next to `phonon_irreps.yaml`; the README figure).
- `crystod-slab`, a new command: surface slabs cut from a bulk structure, every termination of the given Miller indices (three- or four-index, one by one or from a list file) or of every index up to `--max-index` that is distinct under the crystal's Laue group, each tested for a dipole (polar) and for two equivalent faces (symmetric) and written as a POSCAR, with `--max-atoms`, `--no-polar` and `--symmetrize`, and an interactive dialogue when no index is given; `crystod.slab` in Python. Please cite R. Kiribayashi *et al.*, *ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces* **17**, 61872–61887 (2025) when you use it.

- **New: `crystod-phonon --unfold --child-poscar CHILD --child-dim 2 2 2 --parent-poscar PARENT --parent-dim 2 2 2` unfolds the phonon bands of a distorted structure (the child) onto the Brillouin zone of its high-symmetry parent.** Child mode J, computed at the child wave vector K = M k, is drawn at the parent k with the weight P_J(k) of its projection onto the Bloch states of the parent translations (Allen, Berlijn, Casavant and Soler, Phys. Rev. B 87, 085322 (2013)), with the eigenvectors converted from phonopy's actual-position phase to the ideal sites (without that factor, as in phonopy's `Unfolding`, the weights of a distorted child are off by up to 0.14 and depend on the supercell the force constants are written in); degenerate child modes are rotated so that their weights do not depend on the eigenvector basis, and the sum rule (the weights at every k add up to 3 n_parent) is checked on every run. The transformation is not an input: it is the setting that `crystod-group --supergroup-cif PARENT --subgroup-cif CHILD` finds for the two structures (`SymmetryModeAnalysis`), printed as crystod-group prints it (P and the origin shift p) and as the rows of M (`A_child = M A_parent`, `k_child = M k_parent`); `--trans-mat` (nine integers, the crystod-bz convention) chooses another of the equivalent settings (24 for Pnma CaTiO3 and for R-3c AlF3 on Pm-3m), the origin shift following from it, and refuses any other matrix with the list of the valid ones. The path is the parent's seekpath path (or `--band`/`--band-labels`/`--npoints` in parent primitive coordinates); the run prints the child point every vertex lands on, every arm of the stars of the vertices with the arms that fold onto the child Γ point (the condensed distortions, such as the in-phase tilt M3+ of Pnma CaTiO3 on the M arm (1/2, 0, 1/2), which the default path does not pass) and the classes of arms that a child operation or time reversal relates, and the average over the orientation domains (6 for Pnma, 4 for R-3c). Every unfolded level gets its parent irrep from CrystOD's projection operators (the ISO-IR labels `--irreps` gives the parent POSCAR, with the Mulliken symbol at Γ): the irrep holding at least 50 % of the weight of the degenerate group, else the smallest set the child symmetry cannot separate (`SM1/SM2`), else `mixed`; a time-reversal pair of a non-symmorphic parent is one unit (`S1+S2`). The child Γ modes are unfolded onto every parent k that folds onto them, with their child irreps and activities, and with `--parent-dim` the soft parent levels are followed into the child (the M3+ tilt of CaTiO3 at -5.44 THz mainly into the Ag mode at 4.37 THz). Output: `phonon_unfold.pdf` (page 1: parent bands, one domain, domain average and the star arms; page 2: the parent irreps), `phonon_unfold.dat` (two datasets, the path and the domain average), `--export-npz FILE` (every array), `-o PREFIX`; `--child-readfc` and `--parent-readfc` read `FORCE_CONSTANTS`, `--no-irreps` skips the labels and `--weight-min` sets the weight cut. Each force file is read next to its POSCAR, `FORCE_SETS_<POSCAR name>` (the name macer writes) before `FORCE_SETS`, so that the two POSCARs can share one directory, and phonopy runs in an empty directory, so that a `FORCE_CONSTANTS` lying in the working directory is never used in place of `FORCE_SETS`. `--nac` is refused. In Python: `crystod.phonon.load_unfold_inputs`, `find_parent_mapping`, `parent_mapping_from_setting`, `make_parent_path`, `unfold`, `star_arms`, `domain_average`, `irrep_resolve` and `gamma_origin`, with their dataclasses. Example: `example/51_phonon_unfold` (CaTiO3 Pnma and AlF3 R-3c onto Pm-3m, phonopy force sets from the MACE force field), which also ships with the package (`crystod-example 51`; 11 files, 242 kB); documented in section 51 of [crystod-phonon](crystod-phonon.md#51-phonon-band-unfolding---unfold). Tested in the new section 51.
- **New: `crystod-phonon --irreps` now also draws the phonon dispersion with the ISO-IR label of every level at the special points of the path** (`phonon_irreps.pdf`, next to `phonon_irreps.yaml`; the figure of the README, which until now was drawn outside CrystOD). The bands run along the seekpath path of the primitive cell (or `--band`/`--band-labels`/`--npoints`; the vertices of a `--band` path are named by their ISO-IR k-vector type, so a vertex on a line such as DT is labeled too), and the labels are those `phonon_irreps.yaml` holds (`GM4-(3)`, `X3-`, `GM1 + GM5(2)`), read at the vertex itself through the tabulated arm of its star, so every dot lies on the bands it labels (within 1e-7 THz for SrTiO3, ScF3, BaTiO3 and Si). Every point is labeled once, at its last place on the path that is not a break; the two equivalent points of a break with the same levels (U|K of fcc) get one set of labels, two different ones a set on either side; labels of close levels are moved apart (least-squares positions one line apart) and joined to their levels by leader lines; levels below `--threshold` (default -0.1 THz) are red. The figure is a PDF named after the YAML file (`phonon_irreps.pdf`, `SrTiO3_irreps.pdf` with `-o SrTiO3_irreps.yaml`, `phonon_irreps_all.pdf` with `--all-irreps`); the run prints the path and the labeled points in a `* Band path *` block, and with `--nac` a note that the Γ labels are those of the TO modes. The YAML file stays the result of the run: when the figure cannot be drawn, a `NOTE:` line in the `* Band path *` block gives the reason and the run still writes the YAML file and ends normally; a malformed `--band`, or a `--band-labels` count that does not fit it, stops the run with one line before the survey. The README image is now made by `crystod-phonon --irreps -c 221_PPOSCAR_SrTiO3 --dim "4 4 4"`. Documented in section 28 of [crystod-phonon](crystod-phonon.md#the-dispersion-with-the-labels-phonon_irrepspdf); tested in section 28.
- **New: `crystod-phonon --visualize --dim 4 4 4 -c POSCAR` writes a phonon mode viewer: one standalone HTML page that shows every phonon mode at the special points of the k path, grouped by irrep, and `band_vector.yaml`, phonopy's `band.yaml` with eigenvectors along the same path.** The page holds the dispersion along the path of the `--irreps` figure (seekpath, or `--band`/`--band-labels`/`--npoints`) with a dot on every degenerate level of the labeled special points (red below `--threshold`, default -0.1 THz; a click selects the level), one button per special point (`GM (0, 0, 0)`, `X (0, 1/2, 0)`, ...), the mode table of the selected point (band, frequency, the set label as `phonon_irreps.yaml` writes it, `GM4-(3)`, `X3-`, `GM1 + GM5(2)`, the component inside the set and, at Γ, the Mulliken symbol and the IR/Raman/silent/acoustic activity; imaginary rows in red) and a 3D scene in the style of the SALC viewer of `crystod --visualize` (VESTA colors, cell edges, the a/b/c compass, `--bond EL1 EL2 MAX` bonds and coordination polyhedra) with the displacement pattern of the selected mode as arrows on the smallest supercell commensurate with q (`--conventional`: the conventional cell), an amplitude slider (the largest displacement in Å; default 0.25 times the nearest-neighbor distance, 0.5 Å for ScF3 and SrTiO3), an animation of the vibration in which bonds and polyhedra follow the atoms, orthographic views down a, b and c, and a URL hash (`phonon_visualize.html#q=R&mode=1&view=a`) that opens a given point, band and view. The partners of a degenerate level are the symmetry-adapted eigenvectors of `--vector` (`build_symmetry_adapted_modes`); the page receives only the atomic-gauge vector of every primitive atom and the atom list (element, position, primitive atom, lattice translation T) and draws atom (a, T) displaced by Re[u_a exp(i(2 pi q.T - phi))], which reproduces phonopy's modulation on the display cell to 1e-6 (checked by the test suite), so the page stays small (80 to 100 kB for ScF3 and SrTiO3; plotly 2.32.0 is loaded from its CDN, as for the other viewers). The terminal prints the structure, the band path and, per q point, the mode table and the display cell; a display cell of more than 1000 atoms (a general q) gets a NOTE. `band_vector.yaml` is phonopy's own format (`nqpoint`, `segment_nqpoint`, `labels` with the ISO-IR vertex names, the primitive cell, and per q point the frequency and the `eigenvector` of every band, `[re, im]` per Cartesian component and atom); `-o FILE` renames the page only. Options: `--qpoint` (a label or coordinates: that point only), `--band`, `--band-labels`, `--npoints`, `--threshold`, `--conventional`, `--bond` (repeatable), `--nac` (TO patterns at Γ), `--readfc`, `--tolerance`, `-o`; `--bond` is refused outside `--visualize`. `--all-irreps` adds the midpoint of every segment of the path (DT, Z, SM, LD, S, T of Pm-3m; for `--band` the midpoints of its legs) as q points of their own, after the special points: the labels of `phonon_irreps_all.yaml` band by band, a display cell commensurate with the exact fractions of the midpoint (DT (0, 1/4, 0): 1 x 4 x 1 primitive cells), a dot in the middle of the leg, a `Segment:` line, and the page `phonon_visualize_all.html` (`#q=DT(GM-X)` or `#q=5` open a midpoint); a midpoint whose cell would exceed 1000 atoms (the K-GM and X-U midpoints of fcc Si, 4096) is skipped with a NOTE naming the `--qpoint` that draws it, and `--all-irreps` with `--qpoint` is refused. In Python: `crystod.phonon.phonon_viewer_data`, `write_phonon_viewer_html` and `write_band_vector_yaml`, with the dataclasses `PhononViewer`, `ViewerPoint` and `ViewerMode`. The atom enumeration of the display cell and the bond search of the SALC viewer are now the helpers `_display_cell_entries` and `_bond_pairs` of `crystod.visualize_basis`, shared by both viewers (the bond search vectorized; the pages of `crystod --visualize` are byte for byte unchanged); `compute_dispersion` records the q of every labeled point (`LabeledPoint.q`). Example: `example/52_phonon_visualize` (ScF3 and SrTiO3 Pm-3m, the 4x4x4 FORCE_SETS of 28), which also ships with the package (`crystod-example 52`; 7 files, 161 kB); documented in section 52 of [crystod-phonon](crystod-phonon.md#52-phonon-mode-viewer---visualize). Tested in the new section 52.
- **New: `crystod-slab -c POSCAR --max-index 1` cuts surface slabs from a bulk structure: every distinct termination of each Miller index, tested for a dipole and for two equivalent faces, and written as a POSCAR file.** The Miller indices come from `--index h k l` (repeatable; `h k i l` of hexagonal axes with `i = -(h + k)` checked and dropped), `--index-list FILE` (one index per line, `1 0 0`, `1,0,0` or `1 0 -1 0`, `#` comments) or `--max-index N`: every index with `|h|, |k|, |l| <= N` that is distinct under the Laue group of the crystal (its rotations found by spglib at `--tolerance`, and the inversion that turns a slab over), in the axes of the cell used, in pymatgen's order. This is not pymatgen's `get_symmetrically_distinct_miller_indices`, which merges indices under the symmetry of the lattice and, for a trigonal crystal, of its hexagonal setting: on a primitive rhombohedral cell (R-3c AlF3, R-3m Bi) it lists one surface twice and leaves out another, and on a cell whose lattice has more symmetry than the crystal (P1, Pa-3 pyrite, P-3m1, a pseudo-cubic I4/mcm cell) it leaves out surfaces; the two lists agree for a crystal that is not trigonal and whose lattice has no more symmetry than the crystal (cubic SrTiO3 and NaCl, wurtzite ZnO, ...). Exactly one index source may be given, and with none `crystod-slab -c POSCAR` asks for the indices, the minimum slab and vacuum height, the maximum number of atoms, whether polar slabs are allowed and whether to symmetrize them (Enter keeps the default in brackets, `4) quit` exits with 0; the structure file and `--oxidation` are checked before the first question), then prints the `* Equivalent command line *` and runs it. For each index pymatgen's `SlabGenerator` builds the oriented unit cell and stacks whole oriented cells until they reach `--slab-size` (default 10 Å; the atoms span that height less the gap at the cut, so the thickness in the table can be smaller) plus `--vacuum-size` of vacuum (default 15 Å; `--in-unit-planes`: both counted in `d_hkl` lattice planes of the cell), centers the slab (`--no-center`) and reduces the in-plane cell (`--no-primitive`); every termination is listed, `t1, t2, ...` in pymatgen's order, with its shift, number of atoms, thickness (the extent of the atoms along the normal), vacuum, area and formula. **polar** is `Slab.is_polar` (a dipole per area along the normal above 1e-3 e/Å) on the slab decorated with the oxidation states, guessed from the bulk composition or given with `--oxidation Sr=2 Ti=4 O=-2` (they must balance the bulk; all 0 means no ions), and `?` without states (a metal); **symmetric** is `Slab.is_symmetric` at `--tolerance` (default 0.1 Å). `--max-atoms N` and `--no-polar` list the slabs they skip with the reason, `--symmetrize` asks for symmetric terminations (pymatgen's `get_slabs(symmetrize=True)` with the faces compared at `--tolerance`; atoms are removed from one face, so the stoichiometry changes, and a slab left with a net formal charge gets a **charge** column and a NOTE, since "polar: no" then means no dipole, not a neutral slab), `--conventional` standardizes the input to the conventional standard cell first (the primitive fcc NaCl POSCAR gives (1 1 1) polar, (1 1 0) and (1 0 0) non-polar and symmetric only with it; without it a NOTE says that the indices refer to the cell as given), `--orthogonal-c` and `--max-normal-search` are passed on to pymatgen, `--lll-reduce` reduces the in-plane cell with `a` and `b` kept in the surface (pymatgen's own `lll_reduce` LLL-reduces the whole slab cell and can tilt it out of the surface, as on SrTiO3 (3 3 2) with 5 Å of slab and vacuum), and `--output-dir`, `--force` and `--no-files` control the files `POSCAR_slab_{formula}_{h}{k}{l}_t{n}` (`_s{n}` for symmetrized slabs, `_` between the components of an index of 10 or more; direct coordinates, one block per element in the order of the input POSCAR, the slab formula on the comment line); an identical file already there is kept, and a different one is refused unless `--force`. Every run that prints the table ends with the citations of R. Kiribayashi *et al.*, *ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces* **17**, 61872–61887 (2025) ([doi:10.1021/acsami.5c17978](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.5c17978)), the paper to cite when you use `crystod-slab`, and of pymatgen's slab construction (W. Sun and G. Ceder, *Surf. Sci.* **617**, 53 (2013); R. Tran *et al.*, *Sci. Data* **3**, 160080 (2016)). This is the former `script/slabkit.py` of Ryuju Kiribayashi (2024), without its banner; unlike the script, whose `--index` and list-file modes cut one termination per index (`get_slab`) unless they symmetrized, every termination is listed in every mode, and the polar test is pymatgen's dipole test instead of a comparison of the atoms above and below the centre of the slab. In Python: the new API domain `crystod.slab` (`load_structure`, `distinct_miller_indices`, `parse_miller_index`, `read_index_list`, `generate_slabs` returning `SlabSet` / `SlabRecord` with `charge`, `format_table`, `write_slab_poscar` returning `(path, status)` as `crystod.search.write_poscar` does, `slab_filename`, `SLAB_CITATION_LINES`, ...); importing it loads no pymatgen. `crystod-slab --example SrTiO3` / `--example ScF3` run on the bundled cells. Example: `example/53_slab` (SrTiO3 Pm-3m, NaCl Fm-3m from its primitive cell, La2CuO4 I4/mmm), which also ships with the package (`crystod-example 53`; 7 files, 34 kB; with 51-53 the package ships 44 example directories, 243 files, 7.80 MB); documented in [crystod-slab](crystod-slab.md#53-surface-slabs). Tested in the new section 53. The MCP server gains the tool `crystod_slab` (the slab table of a POSCAR for one `miller_index` or for every distinct index up to `max_index`, with the slab and vacuum sizes, `symmetrize`, `no_polar`, `max_atoms` and `oxidation_states` of the command, and the slab POSCAR files only in an absolute `output_dir` the call names; it needs CrystOD 0.4.6).
- **Fixed: the direction column of `crystod-group --supergroup-cif` names each order parameter as `crystod-group --parent` does, by the ISOTROPY representative of its stratum, instead of by the direction the child file happens to sit on.** The irreps are labelled with the ISO-IR tables of ISOTROPY, but the decomposition table wrote the direction of the input child, so a child in another orientation domain read differently from the `--parent SG --irrep IR` row of the same isotropy subgroup (reported on V2O3 R-3c -> C2/c: `GM3+ (0.577a,-a)` against the row `GM3+(a,0) 15 C2/c`; SrTiO3 Pm-3m -> I4/mcm printed `R5- (a,0,0)` since v0.4.1, against the row `R5-(0,0,a)`). The directions are now those of the domain-equivalent child in which the modes, in order of decreasing amplitude, sit on the simplest directions, one parent operation for all rows and chosen by the rule of the `--parent` tables (`IsotropyAnalyzer.canonical_projector`): the largest mode with domain-equivalent directions sits on its `--parent` row, and the rows together still describe one domain (the Pbnm perovskite prints `R4+ (0,a,a)` with `M3+ (a;0;0)`, as in the coupled row `R4+(0,a,a) M3+(d;0;0)` of `--parent Pm-3m --irrep R4+ M3+`, although M3+ on its own is tabulated as `(0;0;a)`). A note under the table, in the terminal and in `sym_mode_<formula>`, names the mode that decides and its `--parent` command, the representatives of the other strata on their own, and the direction the child as given sits on when it differs: `(direction: GM3+ sits on its ISOTROPY representative as listed by crystod-group --parent 167 --irrep GM3+; the input child as given sits on the domain-equivalent direction GM3+ (0.577a,-a))`. Printed outputs that change: SrTiO3 `R5- (a,0,0)` -> `(0,0,a)`, V2O3 `GM3+ (0.577a,-a)` -> `(a,0)`, ZrO2 Fm-3m -> P4_2/nmc `X2- (0;a;0)` -> `(0;0;a)` and BaTiO3 Pm-3m -> P4mm `GM4- (a,0,0)` -> `(0,0,a)`, each with the note; La3Ni2O7 I4/mmm -> Cmcm (`X3- (0;a)`, the row of `--parent I4/mmm --irrep X3-`) is unchanged and has no note. Over 58 structure pairs (the inputs of section 23 and the bug reports), 54 of the 174 rows change their direction label; no other column changes. Unchanged: the sublattice setting (the cell relation, the transformation matrix P and p, the displacement table, the normalized mode components and the VESTA files keep following the input files), the irreps, amplitudes, numbers of modes and isotropy subgroups (those of the child as given; a child on the mirror-image domain of an enantiomorphic stratum keeps its own type, P4_32_12 where the direction shown is tabulated as P4_12_12, and the note names the partner), and the `--parent` tables. In Python, each entry of `SymmetryModeAnalysis.modes` gains `domain_label` (the direction of the child as given) and `representative_label` (the representative of its stratum on its own); `label_info()` keeps its `(label, info, index)` shape and returns the direction shown. Documented in section 23 of [crystod-group](crystod-group.md#23-symmetry-mode-analysis---supergroup-cif). Tested in section 23.

## v0.4.5

Highlights (details in the entries below):

- `crystod --extract-irrep`: the irreps of the electronic bands of one finished VASP run, read straight from its WAVECAR, at the special k points of the file or at every k point, for spin-polarized and spin-orbit runs as well; `crystod.salc.extract_band_irreps` in Python.
- Worked examples with the package: all 41 example directories (README and inputs, without their large or unpublished data) ship in `crystod/examples/`, and the new command `crystod-example` lists them and copies them into a working directory, together with the shared structures and the other directories a README uses; `crystod.examples` gains the same functions. Every example README now runs inside its own directory.
- `crystod-group --parent`: the pair labels of `--kpoint` are accepted by `--irrep`, `--order-parameter` reads sums of parameters exactly, and the direction tables print irrational ratios as decimals instead of small integers.
- Compatibility: `--spin` of `crystod` (for `--extract-irrep`) no longer abbreviates `--spinor`. Test sections run 1-50 (50: the packaged examples).

- **New: `crystod --extract-irrep [PATH]` labels the electronic bands of one finished VASP run with the irreps of the little groups, straight from its WAVECAR** — no crystal-orbital diagram, no sublattice runs, no PROCAR. PATH is the WAVECAR or the run directory (default: the current directory); the structure is the run's POSCAR (`-c FILE` names another file of the same cell and origin). By default every ISO-IR special point of the space group that the file holds is analysed — found on any arm of its star and modulo reciprocal-lattice vectors, at its first occurrence when a band path passes it twice — and the levels are listed by energy: level number, band indices, energy (relative to the VBM, `--zero efermi` or `raw`), degeneracy, ISO-IR irrep (with the Mulliken symbol at Γ) and a note with what the labelling engine flags (an incomplete multiplet at the top of the band list, a numerically split level that was merged, symmetry leakage). Bands within the degeneracy window are always one level, also when the run returns them as pure states of different irreps; such an `A/B` level is classified by Wigner's test of time reversal at k — a time-reversal pair (`-L4/-L5` at L of zincblende with spin-orbit coupling, `DT3/DT4` along Γ–X of zincblende without it), one irrep doubled by time reversal (Kramers pairs at a general point of a centrosymmetric spin-orbit run) or an accidental degeneracy, with the irrep of each band. `--kpoint` takes labels and/or coordinates, `--all-kpoints` labels every k point of the file (lines, planes and general points, named by the ISO-IR k label of the little group), `--window EMIN EMAX` limits the listed levels, `--degeneracy-tol` sets the level clustering (default 1 meV). `ISPIN = 2` runs give one table per spin channel (`--spin up|down|both`); spin-orbit runs are recognised from the WAVECAR and carry the double-valued `-R6+` names of the overlap engine. The report ends with the band edges of every analysed k point and the gap over the file; `-o FILE` writes the same content as YAML (the layout of `phonon_irreps.yaml`). Only the analysed k points are read, one at a time: the four special points of a 135 MB, 288-k-point SrTiO3 band-structure WAVECAR take under two seconds, those of a 3.2 GB spin-orbit CsPbI3 WAVECAR three. The labels agree with the SALC decompositions (`crystod --element O --orbital p`, `--element Ti --orbital d`: the nine O 2p and five Ti 3d bands at GM, X, M, R), with the 62 crystal levels of the anchor engine's level table and with the 154 crystal levels of `--diagram --vasp --vasp-engine overlap` on the same runs. In Python: `crystod.salc.extract_band_irreps(path, kpoints=None, zero="vbm", window=None, spin="both", symprec=1e-5, degeneracy_tol=None)` returns a `BandIrreps` dataclass (`format_band_irreps`, `write_band_irreps_yaml`). The sublattice runs of `--vasp-setup` (Va point-charge species) are labelled too, with the labels the overlap engine gives the sublattice columns (308 levels). Inputs: four small real VASP runs in `example/03_hybridization/vasp_wavecar` — SrTiO3 (scalar, `ISPIN = 2`, spin-orbit) and InP with spin-orbit coupling (the time-reversal pairs); the scalar SrTiO3 and the InP runs also ship with the package (`crystod-example 03`), the `ISPIN = 2` and spin-orbit SrTiO3 runs are not in the pip package (they stay in the CrystOD development tree). Tested in the new section 49 (and section 45 for the API).

- **Fixed: a POSCAR in Cartesian coordinates under a scale factor, or with a negative scale factor, was read as another crystal.** VASP multiplies the lattice vectors and Cartesian coordinates alike by the factor of line 2; a negative factor is the cell volume (the factor is then (|s| / |det A|)^(1/3)), and the line may hold three factors, one per Cartesian axis. CrystOD read POSCARs with three parsers, and none of them did all of that. `crystod.vasp_io.read_poscar` (the run directories of `crystod --diagram --vasp`) scaled the lattice and left Cartesian coordinates as written. phonopy 4.3.0, which read every `-c` file and the unit cell of every `crystod-phonon` mode, does the same, takes a negative scale for a plain factor (the lattice comes out inverted and of the wrong size, for direct coordinates too) and stops on three factors. pymatgen (pymatgen-core 2026.5.18; `crystod-xrd`, `crystod-group --poscar2cif` and `--supergroup-cif`) multiplies Cartesian coordinates by the negative number itself. With scale 1, and with direct coordinates under a positive scale, all three agree with VASP, and all 404 POSCARs shipped with CrystOD are of that kind, so no test saw it. - **Irrep labels: one frame per structure, the same in every command.** An irrep label is defined only relative to a description of the crystal in the ISO-IR standard setting, and the setting leaves a choice (Sr or Ti at the origin of Pm-3m, Si on Wyckoff 8a or 8b of Fd-3m) that permutes the labels at zone-boundary points. Each command used to take the frame spglib returned for the cell it happened to work on (the input cell, phonopy's primitive cell, the standardized primitive cell), so `crystod`, `crystod-phonon --irreps`, `--vibration` and `crystod-mag` could name one irrep of one structure differently. Every command now labels in one frame per structure, fixed by three rules: a cell already in the ISO-IR setting keeps its own axes and origin (the conventional cell, its primitive cell in the standard centring, and a diagonal n1 x n2 x n3 supercell of either, of any size); any other cell gets a canonical frame of the crystal (spglib's frame composed with the lattice rotations and origin shifts of the Euclidean normalizer, the cell's own origin preferred when it is the origin of a standard description, then the smallest sorted list of atomic positions, coordinates compared, and reduced into the unit cell, at 0.71, 0.071 and 0.0071 times the symmetry tolerance, so that coordinates typed with a few decimals cannot fall on a level (5-decimal LaFeAsO and Pmn2_1 cells used to change frame with the origin shift); along a polar axis the origin goes on an atom of the element with the smallest atomic number, centring copies included); a working cell derived from the input cell inherits its frame. - **New quantitative engine `crystod --diagram --vasp`: crystal-orbital diagrams from finished VASP runs.** The third engine next to extended Hückel and PySCF, and the first one that computes nothing itself: `crystod --diagram -c POSCAR --co-left SrTi --co-right O3 --vasp [ROOT]` reads the crystal run in `ROOT/BAND` and the two sublattice runs in `ROOT/BAND_sublattice*` (`DIR/band` when it carries the `PROCAR`; gzipped files are accepted) and draws the same three columns from them. Which sublattice directory is the left and which the right column is decided from the real elements of its POSCAR, not from its name; `--vasp-crystal/-left/-right DIR` override the search. A sublattice run keeps one sublattice and replaces the other by `Va<q><sign>` point-charge species (`Va2-`, `Va4+`) of a modified VASP — a smeared Coulomb charge plus a short repulsive wall standing for the Pauli repulsion of the removed ion's core; VASP is proprietary and is not distributed with CrystOD, the patch is available to VASP licensees on request, and `--vasp` itself needs only the output files. The companion `crystod --diagram ... --vasp-setup [ROOT]` writes those runs' inputs — POSCAR with the crystal's cell and atom order and the removed atoms renamed from the resolved oxidation states, POTCAR split out of the crystal run's (or taken from `--potcar-dir` / `PMG_VASP_PSP_DIR`, with `--potcar-map EL=NAME` choosing a variant), INCAR with `LORBIT = 12` and no `NELECT`, and one explicit KPOINTS holding the symmetry-reduced weighted mesh followed by the zero-weight special points, so one run per directory gives both the SCF and the special-point eigenvalues — and prints the three run commands without starting VASP. Levels, occupations and the complex `LORBIT = 12` projections come from `PROCAR`; each band's projection vector is treated as a vector in an AO-like (atom, l, m) basis and classified with the same representation matrices and character projectors CrystOD builds for a genuine AO basis, so every level carries a space-group irrep whose multiset reproduces the site-symmetry induced representation the report prints (32 of 34 manifolds of SrTiO3 exactly; a best irrep weight below 0.9 is warned about, and on SrTiO3 every level reaches 1.000). The three runs each pin their own G = 0 potential, so the crystal column is taken as the reference and every fragment level is raised onto it by a **site-resolved shift**: one constant per (column, element), fitted to the symmetry-forbidden probes (fragment levels whose irrep has no partner in the other sublattice at that k and which must therefore coincide with their crystal counterpart, weighted four times) and to the pure XPS-style counterparts. The shells of one atom move together, different *sites* do not: on SrTiO3 the Ti site, charged by covalent back-donation in the crystal, has all its levels raised 12.3 eV more than the ionic Sr site, which no single shift per column can describe. The energy zero of the page and the tables is `--vasp-zero vbm|efermi|raw` (default `E − E_VBM`); `--vasp-align rigid` falls back to one shift per column (its numbers are printed as a diagnostic in either mode), `--vasp-anchor EL nl` pins a column on a chosen shell and `--no-align` keeps the raw scales. An occupied-band trace sum rule that uses no projection cross-checks the result, and the bonding/antibonding colouring — which a plane-wave calculation must read from the aligned energies because it has no overlap matrix — is active for every crystal level whose parents sit on sites whose own anchors agree within 1 eV, and suppressed (neutral grey stroke) elsewhere rather than guessed. Output: `CrystOD_{cell}_vasp.html` through the usual writer, a `CrystOD_{cell}_vasp_levels.txt` with every number (per-level shift and the full anchor table included), a machine-readable `CrystOD_{cell}_vasp_levels.json` with the same content, and the terminal report of the other engines. Non-spin-polarized primitive cells only, and every share is a PAW-sphere projection, blind to the radial channel. New modules `crystod/vasp_io.py` and `crystod/crystal_orbital_vasp.py`; documentation: [crystod](crystod.md). Tests in section 3, run offline against `example/03_hybridization/vasp_SrTiO3` and `example/03_hybridization/vasp_CaF2`, trimmed copies of six real VASP runs restricted to the four special k points each, which reproduce every number of the untrimmed runs.

  The PROCAR projections are classified in the Bloch gauge CrystOD builds its permutation representation in, with **no further conjugation**. The engine first conjugated the representation with `Lambda_a = exp(2 pi i k . tau_a)`, by analogy with the PySCF engine, whose AO coefficients really are in the atomic gauge. That conjugation is an exact no-op for every site on half-integer coordinates, so neither SrTiO3 nor ScF3 could test it. Fluorite CaF2 can — its F sites sit on (1/4,1/4,1/4) and (3/4,3/4,3/4) of the primitive basis — and it shows the conjugation is wrong: with it 21 of the 85 labelled levels fall below irrep purity 0.90 (minimum 0.000 at W, 12 failures at L), the fragment manifolds stop reproducing the SALC decomposition, the Ca shell spread is 6.58 eV and the F spread 2.31 eV so both sites fail the scale test, an F 2s anchor is paired with a crystal level 13.6 eV away, and the page carries 0 bonding and 0 antibonding levels. Without it every labelled level of CaF2 has purity 1.000 at GM, L, X and W, the manifolds match the group-theory decomposition exactly, and the page comes out 30 nonbonding / 6 antibonding / 5 bonding. The three half-integer-site systems are numerically unchanged, as the no-op argument predicts. CaF2 is now a test fixture of section 3, and it is also the ionic control of the site-resolved alignment: Ca +4.54 and F +4.43 eV move together to 0.1 eV, against 12.3 eV between Ti and Sr in SrTiO3.

  **The point-charge wall is calibrated, and it is the default.** `--vasp-setup` writes `VACSIGMA 0.5`, `VACRWALL 0.45` and `VACWALL = 2.5 q e^2 sqrt(2/pi)/sigma`, one height per `Va` species so the charge scaling stays automatic; the new `--vasp-sigma` and `--vasp-wall-factor` change the first and the last, and `--vasp-rwall` defaults to 0.45 instead of 0.5. The same values are the defaults of the modified VASP, so a run made by hand and a run set up by CrystOD agree. A 72-point scan over `(sigma, VACRWALL, height)` on SrTiO3 and ScF3 organizes the whole family by one coordinate, the radius `r10 = b sqrt(2 ln(A/10 eV))` at which the wall still repels by 10 eV: below 0.7 A the anion sublattice collapses into the bare `-q/r` well (occupied bands below the projection floor, negative gaps, metallic points), above 1.4 A the wall expels the anion valence shell (an O 2p manifold three times too wide, alignment shifts to +47 eV). The recommended setting sits at `r10 = 0.99 A` for `q = +2` and 1.13 A for `q = +4`, the middle of the 0.85-1.25 A plateau, so ONE setting serves +2, +3 and +4; it is the joint minimum of the anchor-residual rms on both systems (SrTiO3 0.407 eV, ScF3 0.304 eV) and simultaneously the point where the anion p-manifold width matches the crystal best (-0.13 and +0.06 eV, against +0.49 and +0.40 eV before), and it transfers to CaF2 without retuning (rms 0.289 eV). What the wall chooses is the anion column's *position*: across the scan the alignment shift ranges over 45 eV while the residual rms on the plateau moves by 0.1 eV. The shipped `example/SrTiO3_Pm-3m` and `example/ScF3_Pm-3m` sublattice runs were recomputed at the new default (the SrTiO3 anion shift moves from +5.144 to +6.147 eV).

  Two defects the fluorite study exposed are fixed with it. The anchor pool is cut **per element**, with that element's own provisional shift, instead of once per column with a rigid pre-shift: with two sites 9 eV apart that one pre-shift is wrong for one of them and silently drops a whole shell's anchors, so a site could report `shell spread 0.20 eV -> shells agree` when its own level table shows 1.56 eV. A shell that is drawn but contributed no anchor is now named in the report. And the parent energy a bond character is read against averages over **all** parents, with the deep semicore shells excluded (the criterion `--valence-only` uses for the other two engines) instead of everything below a 5 % connector weight: the old cut made both members of one three-level interaction antibonding with no bonding partner at all. The report also names, per k point, the fragment levels the drawn window cuts off the top — an empty cation d manifold can land a few hundredths of an eV above the default `VBM + 10 eV` and vanish at one k point out of four with no message.

  **The forms of `--vasp`, the optional `-c`, and the setting the labels belong to.** `--vasp` now takes **no path** (the current directory is the ROOT), **one ROOT** as before, or **the three run directories in any order** — `--vasp ./BAND ./BAND_sublattice1 ./BAND_sublattice2`, whatever they are named and wherever they live, the crystal run being the one whose POSCAR carries no `Va` species; two paths, or more than three, are refused in one line that shows the accepted forms (naming the three used to be an argparse "unrecognized arguments" error). `-c` is now **optional** with `--vasp`: without it the structure is the crystal run's own POSCAR. With it, the analysis setting is the primitive cell of that file — so the irreps agree level for level with the extended-Hückel and PySCF diagrams and with `crystod -c FILE --element ... --orbital ...` of the same file — and every run is **mapped** onto it: the same lattice (the Cartesian matrices within a relative 1e-3), one global origin shift per run, a permutation of the atoms and lattice-vector wraps, with `Va` atoms mapping onto the removed ones. A `-c` file with Sr at the origin against runs written with Ti at the origin — the same crystal, the origin moved by (1/2,1/2,1/2) — used to stop with `ERROR: ./BAND/POSCAR has Ti where the primitive cell has Sr (atom 1)`; it is now the same analysis, and every number of it (raw and aligned energies, site shifts, projected weights, purities, shares, connector weights) is identical to the analysis in the runs' own setting to the last printed digit. What does differ is the **irrep labels**, which depend on the choice of origin (SrTiO3 at R: 1+ ↔ 2-, 3+ ↔ 3-, 4+ ↔ 5-; at X: 1+ ↔ 3-, 2+ ↔ 4-, 2- ↔ 4+, 5+ ↔ 5-; at M: 1+ ↔ 4+, 2+ ↔ 3+, 2- ↔ 3-), so the mapping is stated in the terminal report, in the level table, in the JSON (`"setting"`) and as a chip on the page whenever it is not the identity. The transport needs no per-atom Bloch phase: the PROCAR projection vector is in VASP's periodic gauge (the gauge the permutation representation is built in, section 2 of the engine report), where a coefficient belongs to an atom and not to one image of it, so the wraps cancel against the lattice sum and the origin shift contributes the one factor exp(−2πi k·s) to every atom alike. Proved on fluorite, whose quarter-coordinate F sites are the only fixture that can see the difference: transported with the atomic-gauge wrap factor exp(−2πi k·n_a) instead, 6 of 85 labelled levels fall below irrep purity 0.90 (minimum 0.249, three levels of one L manifold split 0.249/0.251/0.469), while the bare reordering gives the reference run's 80 levels with purity 1.000 and identical energies. **Names:** without `-c`, or with a `-c` file that lives inside one of the run directories, the page is no longer named after the run directory (`CrystOD_BAND_vasp.html`) but after the compound and the space group — `CrystOD_SrTiO3_Pm-3m_vasp.html`, titled `SrTiO3, Pm-3m`; an ordinary `-c` file keeps the `CrystOD_{cell}_vasp.html` convention of the other engines. If the `-c` file and the crystal run are the same crystal in genuinely different bases the analysis falls back to the crystal run's own setting and says so; if they are not the same crystal, the run stops with one line naming what differs and suggesting `-c BAND/POSCAR`. **`--vasp-setup` follows the crystal run, not the `-c` file:** `-c` is optional there too (without it the structure is `ROOT/BAND/POSCAR`), and the POSCARs it writes are now `ROOT/BAND/POSCAR` with one sublattice renamed to `Va<q><sign>` — its lattice, its origin and its ion order, line for line — with the `-c` file only naming the fragments and the formal charges; it used to write them in the `-c` setting, so a shifted `-c` silently produced fragment runs on another origin and on a lattice constant 3.2e-5 Å away from the crystal run they are compared with, which the new mapping then quietly repaired. A run directory that already holds a finished calculation (`OUTCAR`, `PROCAR`, `vasprun.xml`) is no longer overwritten without `--force`: rewriting it replaced the `INCAR` that was actually run. `example/03_hybridization/vasp_SrTiO3` gained the Sr-origin `POSCAR-finish` of those runs and a README; tests in section 3.

- **Fixed: `crystod --diagram` deleted physical levels in dense sublattices — rocksalt AlN had no Al 3s at the X point.** The extended-Hückel solver excludes Bloch combinations whose overlap eigenvalue falls below 0.2, because their variational energies diverge as (1−K)·h/ε (the overlap catastrophe of diffuse shells). Estimated energies are marked `~` in the terminal and explained in the level's tooltip (and carry `"est": 1` in the page's level JSON); only truly linearly dependent combinations (eigenvalue < 10⁻⁶, no physical content) are still removed, reported separately. The variational levels are bit-identical to before, and in the previously validated materials (ScF3, SrTiO3, BaTiO3, ZrO2) every restored level is empty, so no occupied manifold changed. AlN now shows Al 3s X1+ at X and Al 3p GM4- at Γ with the textbook 3s²3p¹ filling at Γ, and the crystal column gains the previously missing antibonding partners — the lowest empty state at X becomes X1+, matching the X-point conduction-band symmetry of rocksalt AlN in DFT. - The `--modulation` mode table now shows CDML irrep labels (`X3-(1)`, `GM5-(2)`, ...) from irreptables — the same labeling as `crystod-phonon --vector`/`--irreps` — instead of internal block indices (the column header changed from `Irrep Block` to `Irrep`; labels fall back to `-` when irreptables labeling is unavailable, e.g. at non-special q points).
- Irrep and k-point labeling now works at EVERY arm of a special-point star, not only at the single arm tabulated in irreptables/seekpath — across ALL label lookups: the SALC analysis (`crystod -c ... --element/--orbital`), `--atomic-orbital` hybridization, `--visualize`, `--star-of-k` (the header now shows e.g. `X [0.0, 0.5, 0.5]` instead of `custom`), `crystod-mag`, `crystod-phonon --vibration`, `--vector` (q label in the report and VESTA file names), and `--modulation`. E.g. all three X arms (1/2,0,0)/(0,1/2,0)/(0,0,1/2) of Pm-3m, and both X arms (0,0,1/2)/(1/2,1/2,0) of the I4/mmm primitive cell, now get X labels; previously only the tabulated arm was labeled (the SALC decomposition fell back to generic `irrep_N` names). For the phonon commands the k point is mapped onto the tabulated arm (star-arm spectra are identical band by band); for the SALC/orbital analyses, which compare little-group characters operation by operation, the characters are transported by the conjugation isomorphism `h -> g^-1 h g` between the little groups, including the Bloch phase from lattice-translation offsets (verified at the X and W stars of nonsymmorphic Fd-3m Si).
- **Fixed**: special k points with 1/3-type coordinates (K/H of hexagonal groups, ...) were rounded to 0.333333 in the table-derived k-point lists, which silently broke the little-group detection — e.g. the `crystod-mag` survey printed a wrong decomposition with generic `irrep_N` labels at K/H. Table-derived coordinates are now snapped to exact fractions, and k/q-point input across the commands accepts fractions (`--qpoint 1/3 1/3 0`) while near-fraction decimals are snapped (0.333333 -> 1/3).
- **Fixed**: `crystod-mag` exported the SAME spin structure twice (e.g. `..._GM5+_dipole_x_conv.vesta` and an identical `..._x_conv_2.vesta`) when a 2-dim irrep space came out of the projection in the circular complex basis (x + iy, x - iy): taking the real part folded both partners onto the same vector. Such spaces are now recombined unitarily into a real orthonormal basis (possible whenever the space is closed under complex conjugation), so the two components export as orthogonal partners (`..._dipole_x` and `..._dipole_y`), matching the 2-dim order parameter (found for the in-plane Ni dipoles/quadrupoles of I4/mmm La3Ni2O7).
- The `--modulation` default output name is now `MPOSCAR_{q}_{mode}_{irrep}_{subgroup}` (e.g. `MPOSCAR_R_mode1+2+3_R4+_R-3c`; one `{q}_{mode}_{irrep}` group per term in multi-q runs) instead of `POSCAR_modulated`; `--output` still overrides it.
- `testsuite.py` sections are renumbered by command group (1 library core, 2-6 `crystod`, 7-13 `crystod-group`, 14-16 `crystod-bz`, 17-23 `crystod-phonon`, 24-25 `crystod-mag`, 26-27 `crystod-md`; each group closes with that command's CLI regression section), and every `example/` directory is prefixed with its section number (`example/24_phonon_vector` <-> `python testsuite.py 24`). New example directories with captured output were added for the previously undocumented features: `01_wigner_d` (library demo), `04_star_of_k`, `07_direct_product`, `10_basis_function`, `11_generate_basis_function`, `12_show_coset`, and `19_bz_supercell`. See `example/README` for the full map.

## v0.3.0

CrystOD has been restructured into sectioned commands (phonopy style: one main `crystod` command plus per-domain `crystod-*` commands), with phonopy-aligned option names (`-c`/`--cell` for the structure file, with `--poscar` kept as an alias). The old `crystod --<mode>` flat forms were removed; invoking one prints an error with the equivalent sectioned command.

- Mode numbering is now 1-based across ALL commands and outputs: `crystod-phonon --vector --mode`, `--modulation --mode`/`--modeN`, `--vibration --mode-index`/`--component-index` (the component default is now 1), `crystod --visualize --mode-index`, and every printed mode table / Mode Space / component listing. A `0` or out-of-range number is rejected with a clear 1-based message.
- `crystod --visualize` HTML redesigned as an interactive viewer modeled after Henrique Miranda's [phonon website](https://henriquemiranda.github.io/phononwebsite/) (BSD-3-Clause; design imitated, no code copied): sidebar with structure summary, irreducible decomposition, a clickable SALC-basis table replacing the dropdown, lobe-opacity / cell / atom display controls, and a credit link in the page. New `--conventional` displays the SALC in the conventional cell (primitive-to-conventional matrix from the detected centring, as in `crystod-phonon --vector`). New `--bond EL1 EL2 MAX` (repeatable) draws bonds within the cutoff and VESTA-style translucent coordination polyhedra around the EL1 atoms, with boundary atoms completed so polyhedra at the cell edge are not cut off.
- `crystod --visualize` now always writes the interactive 3D HTML, auto-named `SALC_{element}_{orbital}_{kpoint}.html` when `--output` is omitted (previously nothing was written without `--output`).
- `crystod-phonon --vector` usability update: mode numbering is now 1-based, matching the printed table (`--mode 0` is rejected with a clear message); the q-point mode table (space group, q-points, frequencies, irreps) is automatically saved as `phonon_modes_<formula>_<qlabel>.txt`; and omitting `--mode` now exports ALL modes as individual VESTA files (`--mode` still selects specific modes, summed into one file). VESTA file names now carry the irrep label and zero-padded mode numbers (`POSCAR_<formula>_<qlabel>_mode<NN>_<irrep>.vesta`) so that `ls` lists them in order.
- Redesigned the main `crystod` command: the flagship SALC analysis runs without a mode flag (`crystod -c POSCAR --element Ti --orbital d`; hybridization via `--atomic-orbital`), `--visualize` merges the former `--visualize-basis`, and `--star-of-k` stays as the symmetry-information option. `--kpoint` now also accepts high-symmetry labels (GM/X/M/R) in `--star-of-k`/`--visualize` mode — the documented `--kpoint M` form previously failed to parse. Added `--version`; `crystod --help` lists all sectioned commands.
- Added `crystod-bz` (first sectioned command), merging `--bz` and `--bz-supercell`: with the default identity `--trans-mat` it plots the unit-cell BZ with an automatic (seekpath) or manual (`--band`/`--band-labels`) k-path; with any non-identity `--trans-mat` it plots the unit-cell BZ together with the folded BZ of the transformed (super)lattice.
- Added `crystod-md` (MD section) with two modes: `--adp` (replacing `--xdatcar2adp`) writes atomic displacement parameters as a CIF; `--summary` (based on `script/md_summary.py`) reports time-averaged lattice parameters and cell volume with standard deviations over a step range (`--start-step`/`--end-step`). `--dim` now also accepts a nine-value diagonal matrix (quoted or unquoted); `--format` selects the trajectory format (`vasp`; LAMMPS planned). A missing trajectory file is reported cleanly instead of a traceback.
- Added `crystod-phonon` (phonon section), merging the six flat phonon modes as `--irreps` (old `--phonon-irrep`, renamed to match phonopy), `--fatband`, `--lt`, `--vector`, `--modulation`, and `--vibration` (force-data-free). `--dim` accepts three values or a nine-value diagonal matrix, quoted or unquoted; `--band-labels` is the primary spelling (`--label` kept as alias); numbered `--qpoint1/--mode1/...` arguments still work in `--modulation`.
- Added `crystod-group` (representation-theory section) for point AND space groups, merging six flat modes: `--product` (old `--direct-product`), `--table` (character-table display, formerly `--direct-product --show-irrep-table` without irreps), `--decompose` (old `--decompose-irrep`), `--ligand-field ORBITAL` (old `--ligand-field-split --orbital`), `--basis` (old `--basis-function`), `--generate-basis` (old `--generate-basis-function`), and `--coset` (old `--show-coset`). `--pg`/`--sg` are provided as short aliases of `--point-group`/`--space-group`; labels starting with a minus sign (`-43m`, `-1`, ...) are handled.
- `crystod-mag --conventional` exports the symmetry-adapted spin structures in the conventional cell (primitive-to-conventional matrix from the detected centring; `_conv`-suffixed VESTA files).
- Added `crystod-mag` (magnetism section), replacing `--spin-basis`: symmetry-adapted spin bases as the default action, with per-atom spin directions and the noncollinear magnetization input printed by default (no `--show-spin-direction` needed). New `--format vasp|qe` selects the printed input: a VASP `MAGMOM` line, or the Quantum ESPRESSO `&SYSTEM` counterpart (`noncolin`, per-type `starting_magnetization`/`angle1`/`angle2`, with the magnetic element split into one type per spin direction).
- Removed the old flat forms of all sectioned modes — `--salc`, `--visualize-basis`, `--bz`, `--bz-supercell`, `--xdatcar2adp`, `--spin-basis`, `--phonon-irrep`, `--phonon-fatband`, `--phonon-lt`, `--phonon-vector`, `--modulation`, `--vibration`, `--direct-product`, `--decompose-irrep`, `--ligand-field-split`, `--basis-function`, `--generate-basis-function`, and `--show-coset`. Invoking one prints an error naming the sectioned replacement.

## v0.2.3

- Added `--phonon-vector`: phonon-eigenvector visualization as VESTA files with displacement arrows, from the same POSCAR + FORCE_SETS/FORCE_CONSTANTS inputs as `--phonon-irrep`. Modes are listed with frequencies and irrep labels; the commensurate supercell and Bloch phase factors are applied automatically for fractional q-points. Degenerate modes are exported as symmetry-adapted eigenvectors (aligned along symmetry-dictated directions, as in `--modulation`) rather than arbitrary eigensolver combinations. `--conventional` switches the output from the primitive to the conventional cell.
- Added `--decompose-irrep`: interactive decomposition of a reducible representation into point-group irreps from user-entered characters (based on `script/decomose_to_irreps.py` by Hiroki Koiso), with a non-interactive `--characters` option.
- Added `--ligand-field-split`: ligand-field splitting of an atomic orbital (s-i) into point-group irreps (based on `script/ligand_field_spliting.py` by Hiroki Koiso).
- Added `--xdatcar2adp`: time-averaged structure and symmetry-constrained ADPs (U_ij) from an MD XDATCAR trajectory as a CIF (based on `script/xdatcar_to_adp.py` by Ko Sato), with a fast built-in XDATCAR parser instead of pymatgen.
- Added `--phonon-fatband`: element-projected phonon fatbands (PDF per element) along an automatic seekpath k-path, computed directly from POSCAR + FORCE_SETS/FORCE_CONSTANTS via the phonopy API. Colored with VESTA default element colors; `--nac` applies the non-analytical term correction (LO/TO splitting) from a `BORN` file.
- Added `--bz-supercell`: interactive 3D plot of the unit-cell BZ together with the BZ of a transformed (super)lattice, tiled at the |det T| folded supercell reciprocal-lattice points (based on `script/supercell_BZ.py` by Hiroki Koiso).
- Added `--phonon-lt`: phonon bands colored by longitudinal/transverse character with an optional `--nac` correction (based on `script/LT_phonon_band.py`, after Qijing Zheng), using the exact eigenvector projection onto the propagation direction so that diagonal path segments are also colored correctly.
- Added `--spin-basis`: symmetry-adapted spin bases (cluster multipoles / SAMM, after M.-T. Suzuki et al.) with FM/AFM separation, multipole-rank naming, per-atom spin directions with a VASP MAGMOM line (`--show-spin-direction`), VESTA export, and automatic magnetic supercells for q != 0. Omitting `--qpoint` lists the spin-multipole irreps at all special k points (as in the `crystod` SALC survey).
- `--basis-function` now supports the axial-vector components `Rx`, `Ry`, `Rz` (transforming with det(R) R, like magnetic moments), including mixed polar-axial products such as the toroidal `x*Ry - y*Rx`. In space-group mode, omitting `--kpoint` now analyzes all special k points automatically (as in the `crystod` SALC survey).
- Fixed point-group labels starting with a minus sign (`-43m`, `-42m`, `-6m2`, `-1`, ...) being rejected by the argument parser in all `--point-group`/`--subgroup` modes; both `--point-group -43m` and `--point-group="-43m"` now work.
- `--dim` now also accepts unquoted values (`--dim 4 4 4`) in all modes.
- Fixed `--phonon-irrep` with phonopy >= 2.21, where the removed `IrReps.frequencies` property caused an `AttributeError`.
